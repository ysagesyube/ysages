§ I · Who We Are

The Infrastructure for Human Cultural Continuity.

We exist so that

No culture ceases to exist. Because each people, each territory, each language, each technique and each way of understanding the world represents a unique contribution to humanity.

We believe that humanity's most valuable cultures live in relationships: between people and territories, between masters and apprentices, between memory and future. When those relationships break, more than knowledge is lost. Stories, identities and possibilities are lost.

YSAGES exists to strengthen the relationships that keep cultures alive across generations.

§ III · Manifesto
A culture only stays alive when it meets the next generation.
Founding line

Humanity has created mechanisms to protect species, territories, heritage and ways of life. The moment has come to ensure that the cultures that give meaning to human experience continue alive through generations.

Each people, each territory, each language, each technique, each story and each way of understanding the world represents a unique expression of human experience. That knowledge lives in the elders who preserve it, in the knowledge guardians who transmit it, in the educators who share it and in the apprentices who carry it forward.

What we have received from the generations before us must not end with our generation.

§ V · Purpose · Mission · Vision

Three commitments. One direction.

Purpose

Ensure that humanity's living cultures continue alive through generations.

Why we exist.

Mission

Strengthen the relationships of cultural transmission.

Connect peoples, territories, knowledge guardians, educators, researchers, institutions and apprentices so that languages, stories, techniques, knowledge, values and traditions continue alive.

Vision

A world where no culture ceases to exist.

A humanity able to remember who it is, value its many origins and continuously expand the possibilities of its future.

§ VIII · The YSAGES Hierarchy

A living system of interdependent relationships.

No culture exists in isolation. It emerges from interaction among people, territories, knowledge and generations through time.

Humanity
The whole of the living cultures that compose human experience.
Territories
Living contexts where identities, memories and knowledge develop.
Peoples
Communities that share identity, history and their own ways of inhabiting the world.
Guardians
Elders, masters, educators, leaders and transmitters of connections across generations.
Cultures
Living systems of languages, knowledge, practices, narratives, values and cosmologies.
Apprentices
New generations who receive, reinterpret and renew what has been transmitted to them.
Continuity
The capacity of a culture to remain alive through time.
Future
The result of what we are able to transmit.
Structural principle

Without territories, cultures lose context.

Without peoples, cultures lose belonging.

Without guardians, cultures lose transmission.

Without apprentices, cultures lose future.

YSAGES does not exist to store cultures. It exists to strengthen the relationships that allow them to continue alive.

Access levels · § XV

Every piece of cultural content archived by YSAGES receives an access-level classification defined by the originary community at the moment of registration. Golden rule: when in doubt, more restrictive.

Level
Access
Example
Public
Anyone, without restriction
People's name, approximate territory, spoken language
Educational
Pedagogical use in schools, universities, museums
Linguistic structure, craft technique, public narrative
Community
Only within the people + formal partners
Ritual calendar, internal organization, family lists
Restricted
Only with case-by-case authorization from leadership
Specific chants, identified medicinal plants, detailed maps
Sacred
Not publicly archivable; internal custody or blocked
Initiation chants, ceremonial secrets, knowledge restricted by gender or age
